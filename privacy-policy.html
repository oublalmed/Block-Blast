Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Block Blast ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Block Blast does not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App stores game progress data (scores, achievements, coins, settings) locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • IP address (used for general location-based ad targeting)
  • App usage data (ad interaction and viewing data)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

3. Children's Privacy

Block Blast is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertisements shown are configured to comply with applicable regulations.

4. Data Storage

All game data (scores, progress, preferences) is stored locally on your device using standard browser/app storage. We do not operate any servers or databases that store your data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services - for app distribution and updates (Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. You can clear all local game data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it from your device.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
